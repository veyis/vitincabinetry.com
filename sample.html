01 The Atelier

A workshop where timber becomes architecture.

Aldo Marchetti
Founder · Master Joiner

For over fourteen years, Vitrin has designed and installed bespoke cabinetry for residences that refuse the ordinary — from penthouse kitchens to country libraries.

Every commission begins with a single drawing and an honest conversation. We source hardwoods from sustainably managed European and North American forests, hand-finish every panel, and install with our own crew — never subcontractors. The result is cabinetry that ages the way wine does: with character, not wear.

03 The Process

Four chapters,
one obsession.

Our process is unhurried by design. We refuse to rush the conversations that determine whether cabinetry will feel inevitable in its space — or merely installed. Each commission moves through four deliberate chapters.

01 Week 1–2

Consultation

An in-home or studio conversation about how you live, cook, dress, and read. We measure, photograph, and listen — extensively.

02 Week 3–6

Design & Render

Hand drawings evolve into photorealistic 3D renderings. We iterate until every proportion, profile, and finish feels right.

03 Week 7–14

Craftsmanship

In our Brooklyn workshop, joiners cut, plane, and assemble. Every panel is hand-finished in six to nine coats.

04 Week 15–16

Installation

Our own crew installs, calibrates, and reveals. We do not leave until every door hinges in silence.
